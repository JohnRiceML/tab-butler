# Goobi the pixel-pet + in-dock playground
> The X copilot's coral mascot — an ambient mood face that mirrors real activity, plus a tap-to-open Tamagotchi-style playground where you feed Goobi the replies you sent today to "earn" the next page scan.

**Surface:** Lives entirely in the on-page X dock injected by the content script on x.com (`extension/src/content/x-copilot.ts`). Goobi is the dock's header face (and the icon on the minimized launcher pill); tapping the header face springs the playground open *in place of the post list*, inside the same dock. (A parallel copy lives in the side-panel popup, out of scope here.)

## What it does for the user
Goobi is an "honest mirror with a face": his mood is driven only by signals the copilot already tracks — he naps when the feed is quiet, looks around while searching, concentrates while Claude scores or drafts, beams when you send a reply, and goes dizzy-red when you reply too fast. Tapping him opens a small playground where the replies you actually sent today appear as treats you can feed him; feeding fills an energy meter, and once he's pumped he unlocks a "Let's go hunt!" button that rescans the page for fresh reply spots. The point is that a scan is something you *earn* by looking after him, and the celebration is gated so he never rewards an unhealthy pace.

## How the user uses it
1. You open the X dock; Goobi sits at the top as its face, his mood reflecting current state (sleeping / idle / searching / thinking / worn) with a matching status line.
2. You **tap Goobi**. The post list collapses and a playground panel springs open: a bigger, livelier Goobi on a stage, an energy meter, a "🍪 N treats eaten" lifetime stat, and a row of circular treats — one per reply you sent today (each wears the avatar of whoever you replied to; its tooltip is the real reply snippet).
3. You **tap a treat**. It flies into Goobi's mouth, he chomps (heart-eyes), the energy meter ticks up, and a "nom! …" message shows the reply text. Fed treats disappear and never come back.
4. You **tap Goobi himself** to pet him (small cheer + a random line like "boop!"); pets barely move the meter.
5. Once the meter hits "ready!" (a few feeds), Goobi starts doing show-off tricks on every pet/feed and the **"↻ Let's go hunt!"** button lights up. Clicking it collapses the playground and rescans the page; "‹ Posts" backs out without scanning.
6. Optionally, a **"Preview his moves"** chip row lets you fire every mood and move to eyeball the whole repertoire.

## How it works
**Ambient mood (the dock face).** `goobiMood()` (`x-copilot.ts:1676`) computes the mood in strict precedence: transient reaction window → `goobiDrafting` (Claude writing a reply) → `findingSpots` (API search) → `inFlight.size>0` (Claude scoring a batch) → `goobiSearchUntil` (just rescanned) → `repliesLastHour() >= REPLY_HARD_PER_HOUR` (30/hr, from `reply-hygiene.ts:16`) → `opps.size>0 ? idle : sleeping`. `goobiStatus()` (`:1693`) pairs that mood with status copy. Working-state flips update the live face *in place* via `refreshGoobi()` (`:1689` → `goobiDockHandle.setMood(...)`), avoiding a full dock re-render. The renderer is `mountGoobi(host,{cell,playful})` in `lib/goobi.ts` — a 20×14 pixel sprite on a `<canvas>` with a self-stopping animation loop guarded on `canvas.isConnected` + current mood. No network or Claude calls originate in Goobi itself; he only *reads* flags set by the scoring/drafting/search paths.

**Reactions.** `recordSentReply()` (`:955`) fires `goobiReactLove()` (`:1667`) on a healthy-pace reply — a `cheer` that melts to `love` after 1.1s (with an "N-day streak!" line from `replyStreak()` when applicable); a follow fires a quick `happy` (`:1861`); welcome-back fires `cheer` when you open the dock after ≥2 days (`goobiWelcomeBack`, set at boot from `X_GOOBI_SEEN_KEY`). `touchGoobi()` (`:1654`) stamps `goobiLastSeen`.

**Playground.** The header face's `onclick` (`:3020`) calls `togglePlay()` → `openPlay()` (`:2036`), which sets `dockPlayOpen`, **resets session energy** (`goobiFed=0; goobiPets=0`), re-renders, and runs `springOpen()` (Framer-ish height-ease + content overshoot, `:2019`). `buildPlay()` (`:1957`) renders the stage (`mountGoobi(stage,{cell:4,playful:true})`, `:3126`), the meter, the stat, and a treat per `todaySent()` reply not already in `fedEver`. `feedTreat()` (`:1925`) marks the id eaten, animates a clone flying into the stage, then `arrive()` bumps `goobiFed`, sets mood `love` (chomp), and updates the meter. `petGoobi()` (`:1916`) bumps `goobiPets`. Energy = `playHappiness()` = `goobiFed + goobiPets*0.1` (`:1888`); `playReady()` at `PLAY_HAPPY = 6` (`:1887`) unlocks the hunt button (`syncPlay()`, `:1901`). When ready, feeds/pets call `goobiCelebrate()` (`cheer` + a random `trick`). The hunt button calls `closePlay(() => rescan())`; `rescan()` (`:428`) resets the per-session score-call guard, clears `seen`, re-marks visible tweets, and re-scans.

**Persistence.** Two `chrome.storage.local` keys (`lib/config.ts:36-37`): `X_GOOBI_SEEN_KEY` ("goobiLastSeen") holds the last-active timestamp; `X_GOOBI_FED_KEY` ("goobiFed") holds `{ids, total}` — the Set of eaten reply ids (capped at the last 2000 via `persistFed()`, `:1893`) plus the lifetime `fedTotal`. Both are loaded at boot (`:3215-3222`). `treatId(rec) = rec.id ?? String(rec.at)` (`:1892`) keys treats off `SentRecord.id`, a unique per-reply stamp so two same-millisecond replies don't collide.

## What is honest about it / limits
- **Every mood is MEASURED, not faked.** Goobi reads only real signals (drafting flag, search flag, in-flight Claude scoring set, hourly reply count, posts-waiting count). There are no invented "needs" — he doesn't get hungry on a timer, and the docs frame this as the "no fake needs, no dark patterns" tenet.
- **Treats are your real, measured activity.** Each treat is an actual reply you sent today (`todaySent()` filtered by day key); the tooltip/snippet is the real reply text, the avatar is the real account you replied to. You can only feed him what you genuinely did.
- **The pace keystone (the load-bearing honesty guard).** Reply celebrations only fire on a *healthy-pace* reply; past the ease-off line (`repliesLastHour() >= 30`) `goobiMood()` returns `worn` and the reaction window is overridden, so Goobi goes dizzy-red instead of cheering. He never rewards going too fast.
- **Energy is a care/engagement proxy, not a metric.** The meter measures only this session's feeds/pets; it resets every time the playground opens and means nothing beyond "have you tended him enough to earn a scan." Pets are deliberately near-worthless (×0.1) so the earned action is feeding (acknowledging real replies), not idle clicking.
- **The hunt button is the only consequential action** and it just calls the same `rescan()` the dock button does — the playground adds no hidden capability, only a gate.
- **Fed treats never reappear** (`fedEver` persists), so the playground can't be farmed by re-eating the same reply.

## Before you change it
- **`PILLAR`-style single sources of truth:** the mood→CSS-animation contract is split across two surfaces. Every `g-*` class in `goobi.ts` (the `TRICKS` pool, `ANIM` map, idle flourishes) **must** exist in BOTH `DOCK_CSS` in `x-copilot.ts` and the popup's `popup.html`, or a mood will silently render with no animation. Adding a mood is a 4-step change (see `docs/goobi.md` "Adding a mood").
- **Mood precedence order is load-bearing.** `goobiMood()` returns the *first* matching branch; reordering it changes what wins when (e.g. a reaction must beat `worn`, but `worn` must beat `idle`). The pace guard relies on `worn` sitting above the idle/sleeping fallback.
- **The honesty keystone is the pace gate.** Don't let any celebration (`goobiReact*`, the playground `cheer`/`trick`) fire when `repliesLastHour() >= REPLY_HARD_PER_HOUR`. That's the product's core promise; bypassing it turns Goobi into a dark pattern.
- **`resetPlay()` must run on every dock-dismissal path** (✕, pause, relaunch, ambient re-render, teardown) — it's synchronous and destroys the big Goobi handle + cancels in-flight treat clones. `renderDock()` deliberately bails early when the playground is live (`:2985`) so ambient re-renders don't tear it down under the user; preserve that guard.
- **Energy resets on open by design** (`openPlay` zeroes `goobiFed`/`goobiPets`), but `fedEver`/`fedTotal` persist. Don't conflate the two: session energy is the gate, `fedEver` is the "don't re-serve" memory.
- **Persisted-fed ids are capped at 2000** (`persistFed`) — a heavy user's oldest eaten ids age out and *could* theoretically reappear as treats; treat that cap as intentional, not a bug.
- **Not unit-tested.** None of the `scripts/test-*.mjs` suite (the project's gate, alongside `tsc` + build) covers Goobi, the playground, feeding, or the pace gate — they cover pacing/hygiene/targets/etc. Any change here is verified only by `tsc --noEmit` + build + manual eyeballing (the "Preview his moves" chip row exists for exactly that). Regressions in the celebration-vs-pace gate would pass CI silently, so verify that interaction by hand.
- **Canvas loop self-guards on `canvas.isConnected` + current mood** — if you change how handles are mounted/destroyed, keep `goobiDockHandle?.destroy()` before re-mounting (`renderDock` does this at `:2987`), or you'll leak timers / let one animation bleed into the next.
