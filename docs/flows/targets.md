# Target accounts + suggestions
> Goobi's "punch-up" growth mode: track 10-20 reachable bigger accounts in your niche, find their latest original post, and draft a value-add reply when it is genuinely timely — draft-only, pace-gated.

**Surface:** A dedicated dock mode (`dockView === "targets"`) on the ambient Goobi panel injected on x.com, rendered by `x-copilot.ts:buildTargets()` and appended in `renderDock` (line 3149). It sits alongside the Replies/Find-spots and Ideas modes. Follower-count gating depends on the user's own size set in the Goobi side panel.

## What it does for the user
This view contains three deliberately different lists. **Massive account keep-list** retains at most 10 accounts where the user's own settled Fresh Reach replies earned measured views. **Fresh Reach radar** remembers up to 200 accounts—including massive accounts inside or outside the niche—discovered through repeated focused/broad searches. **Tracked targets** remains the smaller practical 2–25× ambient-watch list. The keep-list learns, the radar explores, and Targets continuously polls chosen practical accounts. Every surfaced post still needs a worthwhile individual contribution; size alone is never sufficient.

## How the user uses it
1. Open the Goobi dock on x.com, stay in Replies, and choose **Find people**. Targets is intentionally a secondary discovery drill-in behind warm Comments and ranked reply spots. If you haven't set your follower count, you see a gate telling you to add it in the side panel first.
2. Read the **Massive account keep-list** first when it exists. It shows average/best X-reported views on your settled replies, sample size, and whether the evidence is an early or repeat signal. **Scan winners** runs the full Fresh Reach hunt with one reserved winner lane and one separate new-massive exploration lane.
3. Read the broader **Fresh Reach radar**. It shows saved/massive counts, follower size, peak observed post views, proven-breakout distribution, last check, and strong openings produced. Click **Scan + expand** to discover additional people and rescan a due eight-account slice. The strongest 12 stay compact by default; **Show all** exposes the full saved list, while hidden accounts remain in rotation.
4. Read the "Suggested for you" list for practical-size accounts you may want to poll continuously. Click **+ Track** to add one, or **×** to dismiss it for the session.
5. Or type an `@handle` into the "Track" box and press Enter. Goobi verifies the account exists and is inside the narrower ambient Target band. Massive accounts belong in the automatic Fresh Reach radar instead of the recurring Target poller.
6. For each tracked account, read its standing line ("✓ your replies here beat your average (5) · ↩ engaged back ×2" / "no replies here yet"), then click **Find latest original →**.
7. Review the surfaced post, its freshness label and observed reply competition, then click **Draft a reply ↗**.
8. Edit the draft and click **Review & reply on X ↗**. The exact-post composer handoff remains manual-only.
9. If adaptive pace pressure reaches ease-off, the mode shows a paused banner and Track/Find/Draft are disabled while pressure decays. **Reset local pace meter** is available from the dock menu and popup when the local baseline is stale; it does not reset X.

## How it works
The mode is built every render by `x-copilot.ts:buildTargets()`; state lives in module-level maps and is persisted to `chrome.storage.local` under `CONFIG.X_TARGETS_KEY` via `persistTargets()`.

**Candidate pool (FREE, Tier A).** No fetch of its own — it reuses the `authorReach` Map (`x-copilot.ts:1436`), which is seeded for free when "Find spots" runs (line 492: `authorReach.set(...)` from each search-discovered author's follower count). `buildTargets()` walks `authorReach`, drops self / already-tracked / out-of-band handles via `targets.ts:excludeFromTargets()` + `inReachBand()`, and builds `SuggestionInput`s. Each gets `learnedMult` from `learnedMultForHandle()` (the only signal measured on the user's own reply data, from `aggregateAccounts(replyLog.sent)`).

**Tier-B enrichment (budgeted).** For the strongest ≤8 candidates still missing `following`, `buildTargets()` calls `maybeFetchReach()` (line 2843). That queues a `TWTTR_GET path:"user"` call (RapidAPI X-data, via the service worker) at `REACH_CONCURRENCY = 4`, capped at `REACH_CAP = 80` lookups/session, with a `REACH_FAIL_TTL = 600_000` (10 min) back-off on misses and a budget-error path that frees the cap slot without spending network. On completion it stores `following` + `bio` and re-renders, so openness + niche reasons light up. No Claude here.

**Fresh Reach radar discovery (active, distribution-aware).** Every Fresh Reach click pairs niche Latest with two deduplicated `search-v3 type:Top` lenses: the focused niche query and one rotating exploration query from `freshReachExplorationQueries`. The rotating query is forced to differ from the focused query even on the first click. The rotation interleaves niche topics/keywords with broad operator-free domains such as technology, business, science, culture, sports, and entertainment. Broad terms are account-discovery seeds, not trend or relevance claims. Client-side `looksLikeRT` / `isEnglish` / `isBait` screening runs before authors enter the radar. Clean authors at least 2× the user's size persist under `X_HEAVY_HITTERS_KEY` with a niche stamp, 30-day TTL, and 200-account cap. Entries retain followers, normalized engagement and views, age-normalized distribution, peak observed views/engagement, discovery/check timestamps, checks, latest post ID, and strong-opening count.

**Measured keep-list.** `freshReachShortlist()` uses only the last 30 days of Fresh Reach records matched to an actual reply tweet through RapidAPI, frozen after two days, and carrying X-reported reply views. It rejects flat results with a measured floor, dampens one-off spikes with a two-outcome prior, and caps the list at 10. It is correlation—not proof that the massive account caused the views—and the UI says so. `pickFreshReachAccounts()` builds a due eight-account batch with separate lanes for a keep-list winner and a new massive exploration candidate, then tracked, previously productive, proven-distribution, and Latest-active accounts before priority fill. Accounts beyond the practical Target band remain excluded from the recurring ambient Target poller; their explicit Fresh Reach checks are governed and cached for 12 minutes.

**Ranking + reasons.** `suggest-targets.ts:rankSuggestions()` scores via `suggestionScore()` — a multiplicative sort key (reachFit × openness × nicheMatch × engNorm × learnedMult), missing factors held neutral at 1.0, never displayed — and returns the top 5 minus `dismissedSuggestions`. With `engNorm` now live (from the heavy-hitter discovery), a big *broadcaster* with dead replies ranks below a slightly-smaller account whose posts pop. The band is the research **5–25× sweet-spot** (`TARGET_BAND`, with a fixed `MEGA_CAP`); the biggest get an honest *"big — comment EARLY before it's buried"* cue. Only `suggestionReason()` text is shown.

**Add path.** Suggestions: `trackSuggestion()` re-checks the gate on the cached count and calls `targets.ts:addTarget()` (no fetch). Manual: `addTargetByHandle()` does a live `TWTTR_GET user` lookup (`intent:true`), re-runs `excludeFromTargets()`, then `addTarget()`. Both stamp the owner handle so the list can't bleed across accounts; `ensureTargetOwner()` (called on account-switch, line 3003) resets the store via `freshStore()` if the signed-in handle changed.

**The ambient poller (LIVE, 2026-07).** On rendering the Targets view (unlocked, not paused, key configured), `pollTargets()` runs `selectPollBatch` — ≤5 targets per kick, each gated by a **12-minute per-target TTL persisted on the target** (`lastPolledAt`), so remounts and second tabs never re-pay — and ambient-fetches each one's freshest post. Ambient calls carry `intent:false`, so the governor's conserve mode silently pauses polling as the budget tightens; ease-off skips polling entirely (don't dangle fresh targets at someone who should cool down). **Spike-verified 2026-07: the provider does NOT honor batched `from:a OR from:b` queries** (one author's tweets come back; controls proved the others were active), hence the per-handle path. Live posts float to the top of the tracked list — the point is catching the early window without clicking. Budget: worst case ≈ 5 × 358 KB per fresh kick, amortized hard by the TTL; a typical day is a few MB.

Every successful poll also snapshots the returned originals' views/likes/reposts. When the same tweet changes across two valid observations, measured momentum gives it at most a five-minute freshness tie-break inside the existing live/non-live partition. It cannot cross the 30-minute live boundary, override the reach gate, or turn reply-count growth into a positive signal. No added RapidAPI or Claude call.

**Find post.** `findTargetPost()` runs a `TWTTR_GET search-v3` `from:<handle>` Latest query (count 10), filters to non-reply originals authored by that handle, snapshots their public metrics, takes the newest by `postedAt`, and caches it with reply/like/repost/view counts in `targetPosts` (session map). Explicit clicks stay `intent:true` and invalidate the cached draft; ambient polls never touch your draft. No Claude. Reply counts power the free **early/buried read** (`targets.ts:earlyLabel`): a live post with ≤5 replies shows "only N replies so far — you'd be near the top", ≥30 shows "you'd be buried; wait for their next post", and unknown counts say nothing. Zero extra API cost—the metrics were already in the fetch.

**Draft.** `draftTargetReply()` first sends the post through the shared `SCORE_POSTS` content-fit path, then creates a normal `Opp` carrying the observed author/timing/thread evidence. Its `DRAFT_REPLY` call therefore reuses the same niche, scorer reason/category, author line, Fresh-reach opportunity context, angle, duplicate/repeat-author logging, and measured-outcome attribution as the main queue. The Sonnet result remains cached in `targetDrafts` for editing.

**Pace lock.** `const locked = currentReplyPace().level === "easeoff"`. `replyPaceStatus()` converts locally recorded events into adaptive pressure: lane weights are inbound 0.7, ongoing 0.85, community 0.95, discovery/unknown 1; full weight lasts 15 minutes and decays to zero by 60. Caution begins at 8 pressure and ease-off at 12. These are product heuristics, not X limits. When locked, a banner shows and Track/Find/Draft are disabled until decay or an explicitly scoped local reset.

## What is honest about it / limits
- **MEASURED on our own data:** `learnedMult` / `targetStanding()` measure engagement outcomes across accounts; the massive keep-list separately measures settled X-reported views on actual Fresh Reach replies. Everything about a newly discovered account remains a PRIOR or PROXY until outcomes arrive.
- **INFERRED / proxy:** the ranking weights are a directional prior from the 2023 open-source X ranker, NOT the live 2026 Grok ranker (undisclosed) — stated in both files' headers and in the UI ("computed from what we can see, not guaranteed"). `openness` is the follow-graph shape (following/followers ratio), explicitly described as "a two-way account," never "replies to people" — a high follow-back is just as often growth-farming. `nicheMatch` is a bio-keyword proxy for audience overlap (true mutual-follow jaccard isn't fetchable).
- **Partially measured:** `engRate` (engagement-for-size) is populated for authors surfaced by the radar's rotating `type:Top` searches. For those authors `engNorm` is live and the "posts that hit big here" reason can fire; accounts seen only in Latest retain weaker evidence until a Top result covers them, so coverage remains partial. The rate is a "this account has produced a post that landed" signal, never a claim about typical performance. Follower counts are cached and age, so size is shown as banded multiples ("~7×"), never false precision.
- **Two account-size policies:** massive accounts are excluded from the continuously-polled **Target list** through `excludeFromTargets()`, but retained in the user-triggered **Fresh Reach radar** with a visibility-gap penalty and one bounded exploration lane. A widened major-account window is limited to 20 minutes, 79 replies, and—above the normal <30 limit—live distribution evidence. Their individual posts still need exceptional content fit. Neither path equates follower count with guaranteed impressions.
- **No-auto-submit keystone:** nothing posts automatically. The default exact-post handoff clicks Reply and verifies the draft in X's composer; the user reviews and submits, then explicitly confirms it in Goobi. Fresh reach cannot use the legacy DOM helper.
- **Pace keystone:** the `easeoff` lock pauses the whole mode when Goobi's local product heuristic says pressure is too high. It is not an X threshold and cannot see replies made outside Goobi.

- `extension/src/lib/suggest-targets.ts` — pure, unit-tested suggestion ranking: `suggestionScore`, `rankSuggestions`, `suggestionReason`, `sizeBand`.

## Before you change it
- **The pure logic is unit-tested; the wiring is NOT.** `targets.ts` → `scripts/test-targets.mjs`, `suggest-targets.ts` → `scripts/test-suggest-targets.mjs`. Everything in `x-copilot.ts` (the candidate pool, enrichment budget, pace lock, render) has no unit coverage — the gate is `tsc` + build + the test scripts.
- **The suggestion candidate pool is a by-product of niche discovery.** Find spots, Fresh Reach Latest, and the rotating Top radar searches can all enrich `authorReach`; no separate suggestion-only fetch is needed. Don't add one without weighing the budget.
- **Budget invariants are load-bearing:** `REACH_CAP = 80`, `REACH_CONCURRENCY = 4`, `REACH_FAIL_TTL`, and the `slice(0, 8)` enrichment cap bound RapidAPI cost. The `budget-` error path intentionally decrements `reachLookups` to free the slot. Don't loosen these casually.
- **Two gating layers are separate on purpose:** `excludeFromTargets` (hard membership — can this account be on the list at all) vs `suggestionScore`/`inReachBand` (ranking/fit). Re-check `excludeFromTargets` on any cached snapshot before adding (`trackSuggestion` does).
- **Owner-stamping prevents cross-account bleed:** every store mutation preserves/sets `targetStore.handle`; init (line 3232) and `ensureTargetOwner` reset to `freshStore` when the signed-in handle differs. Keep this if you touch persistence.
- **`engRate` is LIVE but coverage is partial** — it is populated from the radar's `type:Top` evidence, which is what makes `engNorm` and the "posts that hit big here" reason fire. It is sourced from an author's Top posts on purpose (a "can this account produce a breakout" signal); do not reinterpret a low-engagement just-posted Latest result as weak typical performance.
- **The pace lock reads Goobi-recorded replies only** (`currentReplyPace()` over `replyLog`), which is why the UI says it is a local meter — Goobi can't see native replies it did not help record.
- **Drafts use Sonnet (4-6), the costliest call in the flow.** Find-post and reach lookups are RapidAPI, not Claude. The draft is the only paid-LLM action here.
