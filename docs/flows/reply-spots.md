# Reply spots (the core copilot)
> Goobi scans your X timeline, asks Claude to score each post for reply-worthiness, badges the good ones, drafts a reply in your voice on demand, then clicks the visible post's Reply control and prepares X's composer for your review.

**Surface:** Ambient on x.com / twitter.com (content script `src/content/x-copilot.ts`, injected only on those hosts). It paints inline badges on timeline posts, an always-on bottom-right "dock" listing ranked reply spots (`dockView === "replies"`, the default mode), and a closed-shadow-root draft panel. Settings (key, niche, voice, products, and Like + insert) live in the extension popup/side panel.

## What it does for the user
As you scroll X, it quietly flags the handful of posts where a reply is actually worth your time, and tells you why and what angle to take (add value, ask, joke, promote, etc.). When you pick one, it writes a reply in your own voice, grounded in the thread. The default user-clicked action locates that exact article in the current DOM, clicks its Reply control in place, and verifies the draft text in X's composer; only an off-page discovery opens or focuses its exact status tab. You review, edit, and submit inside X. The legacy Like + insert helper is explicit opt-in and never applies to Fresh reach results. It deliberately surfaces only ~1 in 8-10 posts and decays stale spots, so it points you at fresh, reachable, genuinely-fitting conversations rather than every post on screen.

## How the user uses it
1. Scroll the X timeline normally. Posts worth replying to get a colored left border + a compact decision pill such as `✦ Build community · best`. When X explicitly renders `Replying to @you`, Goobi calls the item out as `↩ Comment on your post`, treats it as warm inbound, and carries that relationship into scoring and drafting. If you recently replied to that author, the pill still shows the repeat-author caution; direct comments stay important without hiding the spam-safety signal. Otherwise it shows **Why now**, the three decision signals, evidence confidence, any missing-data caution, and Draft / Open / Mark replied / Skip actions. The scores are decision signals, not reach or spam probabilities. Posts Goobi passed on get a subdued `Goobi passed` pill; opening it shows the angle and content-fit reason, then lets you Add to queue, Draft anyway, or Open the thread.
2. Open the dock (bottom-right) to see the ranked list of collected spots, sortable Best / Recent / Reach / Easy and filterable by author/text. Cards are compact by default: author, one growth move, strength, age, a one-line post preview, Draft, and a disclosure control. A recent-author spot carries a visible `↻ replied 2h ago` chip before you draft. Expand one in place to see the full context, Why this is worth your time, visual Reach / Relationship / Community signals, confidence/cautions, and Open / Follow / Plan DM / Mark replied / Skip. Only one card expands at a time. Best answers the actual decision with four lanes: **Comment on your post** (warm inbound), **Keep it going** (proven relationship), **Build community** (relevant reciprocal peer), or **Earn reach** (fresh reachable conversation); its signals are explicitly not presented as probabilities.
3. Open an in-feed pill and choose **Draft reply**, or click Draft on a dock card, to open the draft panel. It shows "Drafting in your voice…", then a textarea with the reply, angle chips (Connect / Add value / Ask / Joke / Support / Promote — a ★ marks your measured-best angle once ≥2 angles have ≥4 settled outcomes each, `learnFeatures`), a "Steer it" free-text input, Copy, and ↻ Regenerate.
4. Optionally switch angle (re-drafts), type a steer + Regenerate, or edit the textarea by hand.
5. Click **Review & reply on X ↗**. When the post is visible, Goobi stays on the current page, identifies the article by its canonical post ID, activates the comment-bubble/Reply control, and verifies the draft landed in X's composer. An off-page result alone uses the exact-status handoff. It keeps a confirmation card with a clipboard fallback. You review, edit, and submit in X, then explicitly mark it posted; opening or filling the composer alone never increments reply totals. The optional legacy Like + insert helper remains available for on-page posts only and never auto-submits.
6. Optional extras: ⟳ Rescan re-evaluates the visible feed from scratch; **✦ Find spots** searches recent niche posts; **⚡ Fresh reach** expands a saved 30-day account radar, then directly checks up to five practical/massive accounts for newest originals, applies a hard two-hour cutoff, rejects threads with 30+ known replies, and keeps at most one post per author; Pause goes quiet. The minimized pill carries the same manual search controls.

## How it works
**Trigger / scan.** A `MutationObserver` on `<body>` + a URL poll call `requestScan()` (rAF-coalesced). `scan()` (x-copilot.ts) walks every `article[data-testid="tweet"]`, extracts `{id, author}` via `statusInfo()`, skips ads (`isPromoted`), your own posts, in-flight and already-`seen` ids, re-badges cached ones, and pushes the rest onto a `queue` (text capped 400 chars). **At Goobi's conservative ease-off line (≥10 confirmed replies/hr) the ambient scan PAUSES entirely** — no new queueing, no Haiku spend. This is a protective product guardrail, not a claimed X limit. Manual actions still work: ⟳ Rescan opens a 2-minute override window (`manualScanUntil`) and Find spots doesn't route through `scan()`; the ease-off callout says "Goobi paused scanning (⟳ Rescan overrides)". On `/notifications` it diverts to `scanNotifications()` instead.

**Score (Claude, Haiku).** `scheduleFlush()` debounces 900ms, then `flush()` `splice`s up to `BATCH = 12`, increments `scoreCalls` (capped at `MAX_SCORE_CALLS = 40` per page-session), snapshots stats (`snapStats` → `postedAt`, likes, replies via the action-bar aria-label), and sends `SCORE_POSTS` to the service worker. `service-worker.ts` reads the stored niche + products and calls `scorePosts()` (claude-client.ts) → `claude-haiku-4-5`, `X_SCORE_SYSTEM`, `max_tokens 1536`. The model returns `{i, score 0-1, reason ≤6 words, category, products?, anchor?, replyMove?, replyBrief?, risk?}`; enum fields are runtime-validated and product NAMES are resolved SW-side from indices. Back in `flush()`, results land in `seen` (dedup cache) and normally require `score >= THRESHOLD (0.6)` for `opps`; DOM-confirmed direct comments use a 0.4 warm-inbound gate while the scorer still rejects spam/abuse/bot bait at ≤0.2. Below-threshold gets a passed-post explanation with an Add-to-queue override (manual opps are never pruned). Only post text, author, and the observed direct-comment marker go to Claude — timing/reach are computed locally so a stale post can re-rank without a re-call.

**Rank (local, live).** `reply-recommendation.ts` keeps four decisions separate instead of multiplying everything into an opaque “reply fit”: warm inbound (a DOM-confirmed comment on the user's post), discovery (relevance + freshness + observed thread room + reachable audience), relationship (relevance + exact exchanges + measured engagement-back), and community (relevance + niche-peer evidence + bounded reciprocity). The highest lane becomes the row's **Best move**; `priority` only orders rows and is never called an X probability. Missing age, reply count, or audience data receives a middle value and an explicit caution—never the best value. A size-scaled reach ceiling stops a 500× celebrity from beating a reachable account merely because it is large. One confirmed same-author reply within 24 hours applies a strong account-spread factor to cold lanes; direct comments soften rather than hide that penalty because they are real ongoing conversation. Three+ replies in the week remain a fallback when an exact timestamp is unavailable. This is Goobi's anti-spam product policy, not an estimate of X enforcement. `topOpps()` sorts by the active `dockSort` and slices to 25.

The evidence comes from data Goobi already has: Claude's post/niche relevance score; live DOM/API age and reply count; RapidAPI follower/following/bio enrichment; `relationship-memory.ts` exact completed exchanges; `learn-stats.ts` measured engagement-back; and `community.ts` niche-peer classification. The current open-source X system is used directionally: it predicts multiple positive and negative actions from engagement history, hydrates mutual-follow/engagement data, and applies author diversity. Goobi does **not** claim to reproduce that model. Thread reply count is labeled as a competition heuristic; X's `DedupConversationFilter` deduplicates conversation branches and is not treated as proof of one universal reply slot.

**Find spots + Fresh reach (off-page, optional).** `findSpots(mode)` sends governed `TWTTR_GET search-v3` requests, scores candidates through the same `SCORE_POSTS` path, and adds high scorers as normal opportunities. Fresh reach is a persistent account radar. Every explicit click runs the normal niche Latest search plus one rotating operator-free Top query from `freshReachExplorationQueries`; focused niche expressions/topics alternate with broad domains so the radar can discover massive accounts outside the niche bubble. Repeated clicks therefore use different cache keys and have a real chance to discover additional authors while the request governor remains authoritative. Broad results are discovery evidence only: clean original-post authors at least 2× the user's size are merged into a niche-stamped radar capped at 200 entries and retained for 30 days.

The radar stores follower count, observed Top-post engagement, first/last discovery, last direct check, check count, latest post ID, and number of strong openings produced. `pickFreshReachAccounts` chooses at most five due accounts with reserved coverage for a tracked account, a previously productive account, an author active in current Latest, and one massive-account exploration candidate when the pool supports them; remaining slots fill by priority. It then runs the provider-required per-handle `from:<handle>` searches with concurrency capped at three, refreshes shared Target data, records scan yield, and merges newest originals into the broad Latest pool. The Find-people view lists the strongest 12 saved radar accounts—including massive accounts—with check/opening evidence, plus a **Show all** control for manually scanning the full saved set; collapsed accounts remain in automatic rotation.

The post gate remains separate from account sourcing: known age no older than two hours, account at least 2× the user's size, fewer than 30 known replies, a 24h/72h same-author penalty, and one post per author. Unknown reply count is rejected rather than mislabeled “uncrowded.” The practical audience band receives the best fit; accounts beyond it—including >500k accounts—remain eligible with a logarithmic audience-gap penalty. A massive account can therefore win when its post is exceptionally fresh, open, and relevant, but size alone cannot crown it. Claude must then clear the stricter 0.68 Fresh content gate, name the exact post anchor and a useful contribution, and report no generic/promotional/context risk. All numeric bands, windows, and cutoffs are inspectable Goobi heuristics—not X guarantees. Premium tier is deliberately absent from selection and scoring: X documents only a slight verified-reply preference in conversation ranking, not a For You or impressions guarantee. The monthly governor can reduce discovery/direct checks, and each exact query is cached for 12 minutes.

Each hunt leaves a visible receipt with accounts checked, originals inspected, timing/room matches, strong content openings, and any budget limit. Every Fresh card recomputes age, known reply competition, audience band, and same-author spread when it renders or drafts; an expired result remains available but changes to **cooled** and loses the timing claim. The expanded card shows the observed age, replies, and audience multiple. Its selection snapshot—including account source, sourcing priority, observed opportunity, and content fit—is copied into a confirmed `SentRecord`, which makes later outcome analysis possible without pretending the initial heuristic caused the result.

After content scoring, Fresh Reach computes one comparative opening index: `contentFit × liveObservedOpportunity × boundedMomentum`. `liveObservedOpportunity` already includes freshness, thread room, the plateaued reachable-audience factor, and author spread; measured public-post momentum can add at most 12%. Weak content therefore cannot be rescued by a large account or a fast-moving post. On **Best**, the strongest live Fresh candidate is pinned once above the normal queue as **Best observed reach opening now**; **Reach** sorts by the same index instead of raw follower count. The hero shows age, observed replies, audience multiple, and content fit, and explicitly describes the index as directional rather than an impression forecast.

The expanded Replies header exposes **✦ Find spots** and **⚡ Fresh reach** together; **↻ Rescan this page** lives in the overflow because it only re-evaluates rendered feed posts. Fresh reach is disabled until the user's handle/follower count is known, because “reachable” cannot honestly be inferred without a baseline.

**Measured opportunity momentum.** The selected original posts returned by Find Spots (up to 18) and target-account polling are folded into a compact public-post metric ledger keyed by tweet ID. Two changed observations 5–120 minutes apart produce a short-lived velocity signal from view/like/repost deltas; reply growth is excluded because it is competition. The signal adds at most +12% to **Best-sort order only** and can show `↗ picking up`. It never changes the displayed/recorded fit score, the 0.6 eligibility gate, other sorts, or the learning-loop correlation axis. Identical cached responses do not advance the observation clock; tracks expire after 48 hours and the store caps at 300. Cross-tab updates union by ID and retain the newer observation pair.

**Draft (Claude, Sonnet).** `openDraftFromEl()` / a dock click → `draftFor(req)`. It resolves the promote product, paints the loading panel, and sends `DRAFT_REPLY {author, text, context, angle, product, steer, threadLine, opportunityLine}`. `service-worker.ts` reads stored voice + niche and calls `draftReply()` (claude-client.ts) → `claude-sonnet-4-6`, `X_DRAFT_SYSTEM`, `max_tokens 400`, returning plain text (not JSON). The user message carries a **grounding context block** (`draft-context.ts:buildDraftContext` — niche, scorer reason/category, honest author evidence, relationship context, and observed opportunity context). Fresh-reach grounding tells the drafter to write one self-contained contribution for surrounding readers and never expose the tactic by mentioning account size, Premium, a blue check, reach, or impressions. Target-account drafts now score the post first, create a shared `Opp`, and use this same enriched context/logging path instead of the former bare `{author,text}` call.

**User-clicked reply action (never posts).** `runReplyDraftAction(text)` defaults to `handoffReply`, but it is local-first: `findPost()` resolves the exact visible article synchronously, `startVisiblePostReview()` clicks its `[data-testid="reply"]`, accessible `Reply` control, or the scoped current X comment-bubble glyph, and the flow accepts success only when the normalized composer text equals the draft. That path never opens another tab. Only when the exact article is absent does the source send `OPEN_REPLY_HANDOFF`; the service worker validates/stores a five-minute one-shot record, focuses an existing matching status tab or creates the canonical `x.com/<author>/status/<id>` tab, and the destination claims only the record matching its URL. Occupied composers are never overwritten. The confirmation card still requires explicit **Mark as posted** confirmation. `X_REPLY_INSERT_KEY === true` preserves the legacy on-page Like + insert helper for an explicit opt-in, but `source: "fresh-reach"` always stays manual. No path submits the reply.

**State / persistence.** `recordSentReply()` → `logSentReply()` appends a `SentRecord` to `replyLog.sent` (capped `SENT_MAX = 500`), bumps `replyLog.daily`/`total`/`times`, updates the recorded author's last-reply timestamp, adds the post id to `commentedIds` (drives the green `✓ Replied` overlay), and persists the whole `replyLog` to `chrome.storage.local` under `X_REPLY_LOG_KEY`. A successful insert starts unconfirmed; copy/open confirmation is stamped manual. That timestamp feeds the next pre-draft recommendation. `recordReplyAndNudge()` also updates the normalized-draft dedup list and returns one record-time nudge via `pickReplyNudge`. Pause state persists under `X_PAUSED_KEY`. The `seen`/`opps` maps are in-memory per page-session.

## What is honest about it / limits
- **No automated cold engagement is the keystone.** Fresh-reach and other default handoffs use X's official composer and require manual review, submission, and confirmation. The legacy DOM helper is off by default and never runs for Fresh reach.
- **Pace / reputation guards are real and cross-session** (X penalties attach to the account): hourly volume (`replyLog.times` vs `REPLY_HARD_PER_HOUR`), same-author spread (`AUTHOR_REPEAT_TTL` 3 days), and duplicate-reply detection (`isDuplicateReply` over recent normalized drafts). Follows are paced too (`FOLLOW_MIN_GAP_MS` 20s jittered + `FOLLOW_HOUR_CAP` 15/hr) and `followAuthor` can only follow, never unfollow. Goobi's mood deliberately refuses to celebrate a reply once you're past the hourly line.
- **Copy/open handoffs require confirmation; successful fills do not.** Copying or opening a post counts nothing until **Mark as posted**. A successful Like + insert is recorded immediately as a pending attempt and later matched through RapidAPI when available. If the user abandons that filled X draft, local attempt totals can temporarily overcount; verified analytics continue to distinguish confirmed from pending records.
- **Score split: model = content/fit only; policy = the user's next move.** Haiku judges the post's text + niche fit and ignores recency/engagement. `recommendReply` then combines observed opportunity and relationship evidence into three inspectable decision scores. Following/follower ratio remains a bounded fallback; exact exchanges and measured engagement-back outrank it. Fixed freshness, competition, and reach curves are product priors, not claims about hidden X weights.
- **Outcome learning IS wired (best-effort).** `runMeasurePass`, called through `maybeRunDailyLearn`, pulls recent replies via `user-replies-v2`, matches each back to a stored `SentRecord` (`matchOutcomes`), and writes real views/likes/replies/reposts into `SentRecord.outcome`; those feed `aggregateAccounts` → the per-account "who you show up with" scores. A new successful insert gets one verification attempt after six minutes; outcome refreshes remain daily. Caveats: this needs a Twttr key and a Tier-2 measured score only appears after `N_MIN_OUT = 4` outcomes. The learned reply-queue tilt remains behind its reversible flag and correlation gate; thin or non-predictive data stays neutral.
- **Every opportunity uses one explicit user action.** Search-discovered and Fresh reach posts always use the official manual composer and require explicit posted confirmation. Opening or pre-filling the composer is never counted as posting.
- **DOM-scraped, best-effort.** Engagement counts, avatars, verified badges, and display names come from X's DOM (quote-tweet-aware: always the OUTER post). Anything unreadable is simply omitted, never guessed.
- **Per-session cost cap.** `MAX_SCORE_CALLS = 40` bounds Claude spend/ToS exposure per page; hitting it surfaces a one-time toast pointing at ⟳ Rescan (which resets the cap and clears `seen`).

## Key files
- `src/content/x-copilot.ts` — the content-script pipeline: scan/flush, badge/addButton, the dock (`renderList`/`topOpps`/`recommendationFor`), API enrichment, findSpots, drafting, Like + insert / copy + open routing, and reply logging.
- `src/lib/reply-recommendation.ts` — pure three-lane recommendation policy, component evidence, confidence, reasons, and cautions. Covered by counterfactual tests in `scripts/test-reply-recommendation.mjs`.
- `src/lib/fresh-reach.ts` — pure larger-account sourcing plus hard post eligibility and observed-opportunity ordering for just-posted, reachable, uncrowded, account-diverse results. Covered by `scripts/test-fresh-reach.mjs`.
- `src/lib/prompts.ts` — `X_SCORE_SYSTEM`, `X_DRAFT_SYSTEM`, and `REPLY_ANGLES` (the 6 angle directives + labels used for badge tags and the panel chips).
- `src/lib/claude-client.ts` — `scorePosts` (Haiku, JSON), `draftReply` (Sonnet, plain text), `stripDashes` (dash/hyphen safety net), `callDirect`/`rawCall` (BYO-key direct Anthropic fetch).
- `src/background/service-worker.ts` — `SCORE_POSTS` and `DRAFT_REPLY` message handlers (inject stored niche/voice/products), plus `GET_FAVICONS` and the governed `TWTTR_GET`.
- `src/lib/reply-hygiene.ts` — `isDuplicateReply`, `normalizeReply`, `pickReplyNudge`, `REPLY_HARD_PER_HOUR` (the pace/dedup guards). `src/lib/human-pacing.ts` — `humanDelayMs`, `jitterGap`.

## Before you change it
- **Two Claude models, two cost tiers, on purpose:** scoring is Haiku (cheap, batched, JSON, capped at 40 calls/session), drafting is Sonnet (quality, one call per draft, plain text). Don't collapse them or switch the draft to JSON — `draftReply` returns free-form prose precisely because JSON-wrapping reply text is fragile.
- **Eligibility and ordering are different decisions.** `THRESHOLD = 0.6` remains a raw model-fit gate. Eligible posts then run through `recommendReply`; its priority drives Best sort while the lane/reasons drive the row copy. Optional measured per-account learning and momentum are bounded sort-only tilts. Editing the prompt changes relevance eligibility; editing `reply-recommendation.ts` changes where the user's time is spent.
- **The user-click guarantees are load-bearing trust features.** Keep the visible-post path in the current tab, the off-page-only handoff, Fresh reach's manual-only boundary, explicit confirmation, and hourly/duplicate/spread nudges. Never auto-submit or add word-by-word synthetic typing.
- **X's virtualized timeline + closed shadow roots:** dedup is by status id, overlays are idempotent and re-applied from the `seen` cache on remount, and an open overlay reads its latest WeakMap model instead of retaining stale recommendation data. DOM writes are conditional because the body observer sees article descendants. `findPost` is strict id-only (never a same-author lookalike), and panel inputs live in closed shadow roots with `trapKeys` so X's single-key shortcuts don't hijack typing. Respect these when adding DOM logic.
- **Tests:** `scripts/test-reply-recommendation.mjs` asserts reachable-vs-mega, known-vs-missing competition, crowding, exact connections, poor-fit protection, peers, thin reply-back evidence, freshness, and author diversity. The clipboard/new-tab/confirmation wiring still relies on `tsc --noEmit` + build + manual on-page testing.
- **`SentRecord.outcome` IS written and read** — `runMeasurePass` writes it (`x-copilot.ts:2198`) and `aggregateAccounts` reads it (`learn-stats.ts:108,121`). It's sparse (needs a Twttr key + ≥4 settled outcomes per account before a measured score shows), but it is NOT dead weight — don't re-stub it. This is the data that makes the audit's "measure-first" tests runnable on data already in hand.
