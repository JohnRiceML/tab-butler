# Reply spots (the core copilot)
> Goobi scans your X timeline, asks Claude to score each post for reply-worthiness, badges the good ones, and drafts a reply in your voice on demand. It copies the draft, but you operate X's Reply control and composer yourself.

**Surface:** Ambient on x.com / twitter.com (content script `src/content/x-copilot.ts`, injected only on those hosts). It paints inline badges on timeline posts, an always-on bottom-right "dock" listing ranked reply spots (`dockView === "replies"`, the default mode), and a closed-shadow-root draft panel. Settings (key, niche, voice, and products) live in the extension popup/side panel.

## What it does for the user
As you scroll X, it quietly flags the handful of posts where a reply is actually worth your time, and tells you why and what angle to take (add value, ask, joke, promote, etc.). When you pick one, it writes a reply in your own voice, grounded in the thread. The user-clicked action only copies the draft. For an off-page discovery it also opens or focuses the exact status page. You click Reply, paste, review, and submit inside X yourself. Goobi has no auto-like, reply-click, composer-fill, or submit mode. Each post is assessed independently, with no acceptance quota. A relevant feed can produce several useful opportunities; stale spots still decay in the local ranking.

## How the user uses it
1. Scroll the X timeline normally. Posts worth replying to get a colored left border + a compact decision pill such as `✦ Build community · best`. When X explicitly renders `Replying to @you`, Goobi calls the item out as `↩ Comment on your post`, treats it as warm inbound, and carries that relationship into scoring and drafting. If you recently replied to that author, the pill still shows the repeat-author caution; direct comments stay important without hiding the spam-safety signal. Otherwise it shows **Why now**, the three decision signals, evidence confidence, any missing-data caution, and Draft / Open / Mark replied / Skip actions. The scores are decision signals, not reach or spam probabilities. Posts Goobi passed on get a subdued `Goobi passed` pill; opening it shows the angle and content-fit reason, then lets you Add to queue, Draft anyway, or Open the thread.
2. Open the dock (bottom-right) to see the ranked list of collected spots, sortable Best / Recent / Reach / Easy and filterable by author/text. Cards are compact by default: author, one growth move, strength, age, a one-line post preview, Draft, and a disclosure control. A small ↗ badge appears on the avatar only when **Review & reply** will need to open the exact post on a separate X page because it is not currently available in the feed; no badge means Goobi can use the visible feed post in place. The avatar remains part of the card disclosure rather than pretending every face is a profile link. A direct comment on your post gets a green **“They replied to your post · Reply back in your thread”** banner even while collapsed, and its primary action reads **Reply back**. A recent-author spot carries a visible `↻ replied 2h ago` chip before you draft. Expand one in place to see the full context, Why this is worth your time, visual Reach / Relationship / Community signals, confidence/cautions, and Open / Follow / Plan DM / Mark replied / Skip. Only one card expands at a time. Best answers the actual decision with four lanes: **Comment on your post** (warm inbound), **Keep it going** (proven relationship), **Build community** (relevant reciprocal peer), or **Earn reach** (fresh reachable conversation); its signals are explicitly not presented as probabilities.
3. Open an in-feed pill and choose **Draft reply**, or click Draft on a dock card, to open the draft panel. It shows "Drafting in your voice…", then a textarea with the reply, angle chips (Connect / Add value / Ask / Joke / Support / Promote — a ★ marks your measured-best angle once ≥2 angles have ≥4 settled outcomes each, `learnFeatures`), the separate **Community spark** delivery-style control, a "Steer it" free-text input, Copy, and ↻ Regenerate.
4. Optionally switch angle, toggle **Community spark**, type a steer + Regenerate, or edit the textarea by hand. Angle decides the substantive move. Community Spark is on by default; turning it off or back on saves that choice locally for future feed, dock, Fresh Reach, and target-account drafts across tabs and browser restarts. It keeps the selected move but compresses it into one short, post-specific insight or natural bit of wit, with a narrow invitation only when it genuinely deepens the conversation.
5. Click **Copy & scroll to post** for a post currently visible in the feed, or **Copy & open exact post ↗** when the avatar carries the off-page badge. The in-feed path smoothly centers the exact matched article and gives it a temporary accent outline, but leaves X's controls untouched. The off-page path opens or focuses only the matching status page. In both cases you click X's Reply button, paste, review, and submit manually, then explicitly mark it posted in Goobi. Copying, scrolling, highlighting, or opening never increments reply totals.
6. Optional extras: ⟳ Rescan re-evaluates the visible feed from scratch; **✦ Find spots** searches recent niche posts; **⚡ Fresh reach** expands a saved 30-day account radar, then directly checks up to 24 practical/massive accounts for newest originals. Up to four due lanes can come from the private massive-account watchlist. The scan applies a hard two-hour cutoff, rejects crowded normal openings, lets at most two posts per author reach Claude, and keeps only the best eligible post per author after content scoring; Pause goes quiet. The minimized pill carries the same manual search controls.

## How it works
**Trigger / scan.** A `MutationObserver` on `<body>` + a URL poll call `requestScan()` (rAF-coalesced). `scan()` walks every `article[data-testid="tweet"]`, extracts `{id, author}`, skips ads, the user's own posts, in-flight/already-seen ids, and pushes the rest onto the scoring queue. **At Goobi's adaptive ease-off line (12 pace pressure) ambient scan pauses**—no new queueing or Haiku spend. Pressure counts cold discovery replies fully, warm/ongoing conversations less, and begins decaying after 15 minutes. Manual actions still work: ⟳ Rescan opens a two-minute override; **Reset local pace meter** starts a new Goobi baseline without deleting activity history or resetting X. On `/notifications` the route instead harvests inbound comments.

**Score (Claude).** `scheduleFlush()` starts a 900ms timer that feed mutations cannot postpone. One ambient batch runs at a time, with up to `BATCH = 12` unique live posts and `MAX_SCORE_CALLS = 40` per page-session. `flush()` rereads text, quoted context and the direct-comment marker immediately before dispatch, snapshots stats, and sends `SCORE_POSTS`. The service worker adds the stored focus and products; `scorePosts()` applies the same independent content-fit criteria to every post, whether supplied alone or in a batch. Output tokens scale with batch size. Every requested index must have exactly one finite score and a reason; missing, duplicate or malformed rows fail the scan and receive one bounded retry, never a fabricated pass.

Results enter `seen` with a fingerprint of the actual scored input. Changed text, quote context or direct-comment evidence invalidates the earlier read. Rescan and focus/product changes invalidate older in-flight assessments. A recycled live row cannot receive a result belonging to its previous post. Normal opportunities require raw fit >= 0.6; direct comments use 0.4. Timing and audience remain local ranking signals. **Add to queue** pins the post and reuses its assessment when the content is unchanged; unread or changed content is scored once through the same path. Low-fit manual pins say **Added by you**, retain their real score, and survive rescans.

**Rank (local, live).** `reply-recommendation.ts` keeps four decisions separate instead of multiplying everything into an opaque “reply fit”: warm inbound (a DOM-confirmed comment on the user's post), discovery (relevance + freshness + observed thread room + reachable audience), relationship (relevance + exact exchanges + measured engagement-back), and community (relevance + niche-peer evidence + bounded reciprocity). The highest lane becomes the row's **Best move**; `priority` only orders rows and is never called an X probability. Missing age, reply count, or audience data receives a middle value and an explicit caution—never the best value. A size-scaled reach ceiling stops a 500× celebrity from beating a reachable account merely because it is large. One confirmed same-author reply within 24 hours applies a strong account-spread factor to cold lanes; direct comments soften rather than hide that penalty because they are real ongoing conversation. Three+ replies in the week remain a fallback when an exact timestamp is unavailable. This is Goobi's anti-spam product policy, not an estimate of X enforcement. `topOpps()` sorts by the active `dockSort` and slices to 25.

The evidence comes from data Goobi already has: Claude's post/niche relevance score; live DOM/API age and reply count; RapidAPI follower/following/bio enrichment; `relationship-memory.ts` exact completed exchanges; `learn-stats.ts` measured engagement-back; and `community.ts` niche-peer classification. The current open-source X system is used directionally: it predicts multiple positive and negative actions from engagement history, hydrates mutual-follow/engagement data, and applies author diversity. Goobi does **not** claim to reproduce that model. Thread reply count is labeled as a competition heuristic; X's `DedupConversationFilter` deduplicates conversation branches and is not treated as proof of one universal reply slot.

**Find spots + Fresh reach (off-page, optional).** `findSpots(mode)` sends governed `TWTTR_GET search-v3` requests, scores candidates through the same `SCORE_POSTS` path, and adds high scorers as normal opportunities. Fresh reach is a persistent account radar. Every explicit click runs niche Latest plus three deduplicated Top lenses: the focused niche expression and up to two rotating operator-free exploration queries from `freshReachExplorationQueries`. The rotation includes niche topics and broad domains so the radar can discover major distribution outside the existing niche bubble. Repeated clicks therefore use different cache keys and have a real chance to discover additional authors while the request governor remains authoritative. Broad results are account-discovery evidence only: clean original-post authors at least 2× the user's size are merged into an owner- and niche-stamped radar capped at 400 entries and retained for 30 days.

The radar stores follower count, audience-normalized engagement/view rates, an age-normalized distribution score, recent peak views/engagement with per-metric timestamps, first/last discovery, last direct check, check count, latest post ID, a bounded opening-ID dedupe ledger, and an independent cumulative opening total. Old public distribution evidence decays with a 14-day half-life instead of becoming permanent, and a weak sighting cannot re-age an old peak. The first Top query is always niche-focused; rotating Top queries are forced to differ from it, so every hunt also expands account coverage. `pickFreshReachAccounts` chooses at most 24 due accounts. Its account score gives only 8% to raw audience size and 6% to normalized engagement rate, while normalized/velocity distribution plus a separate log-scaled absolute peak-view/peak-engagement signal receive 44%. Up to four rotating lanes go to explicit private watchlist pins; measured massive-account winners and up to three new evidence-backed massive accounts retain separate bounded coverage. A massive row's historical yield must remain at least neutral to reserve a lane. Tracked, productive, proven-distribution, and Latest-active signals otherwise contribute to priority rather than each claiming a hard slot, and an author replied to in the last 24 hours cannot force a reserved check. Fame alone cannot win a massive exploration lane. Per-handle `from:<handle>` searches run with concurrency capped at six, refresh shared Target and radar data, and credit opening yield only for new non-cache checks. Provider-wide auth, quota, rate, timeout, and outage failures stop later batches without poisoning individual account backoffs. A query-specific Latest failure can fall back to saved accounts. The Find-people view separates a private max-12 massive watchlist from practical Targets, shows a measured-account funnel, and lists the strongest saved radar accounts with recent peak-view, peak-engagement, and check/opening evidence.

**Massive account keep-list.** `freshReachShortlist()` derives at most 10 accounts from the last 30 days of the existing reply ledger—no new endpoint or invented outcome. A row must be a Fresh Reach reply to a massive account, matched to an actual reply tweet through RapidAPI, at least two days old/frozen, and carry an X-reported reply-view count. A fixed/global measured floor removes flat results; a two-outcome prior dampens one lucky spike. The UI shows average/best reply views, settled sample size, and early-vs-repeat signal, and explicitly frames this as correlation rather than proof that the account caused the views. These winners feed the first direct-check lane; the separate three-lane evidence-backed exploration allocation prevents the system from becoming a closed loop around old winners.

The post gate remains separate from account sourcing: every candidate needs known age no older than two hours, a metric snapshot no older than six minutes, an account at least 2× the user's size, known reply count, and a 24h/72h same-author penalty. It then assigns one honest opportunity class. **Early + open** retains the normal <30-reply rule. **Breakout pace** requires exceptional age-normalized public views or likes/reposts/quotes and may remain eligible through 49 replies for at most 90 minutes. **Major account · early** is limited to the first 20 minutes and at most 79 replies; above the normal room limit it also requires live distribution evidence, so fame alone cannot widen the gate. Opportunity is monotonic across classification thresholds: earning a breakout/major label cannot lower an otherwise identical normally-open post. Unknown reply count is always rejected. Already-reviewed rows are refreshed without consuming Claude work; the novel max-36 batch retains up to two posts per author and reserves recall for both practical and massive accounts before filling globally. Only the best strict `content × opening` result per author survives. Claude must clear the stricter 0.68 Fresh content gate, name the exact post anchor and a useful contribution, and report no generic/promotional/context risk. Target-card reads carry the governor's original cache timestamp into the same six-minute observation gate; reopening a cached result can no longer make it look newly observed. All numeric bands, windows, and cutoffs are inspectable Goobi heuristics—not X guarantees. Premium tier is deliberately absent from selection and scoring: X documents only a conversation-ranking preference, not a For You or impressions guarantee. A fully cold hunt makes at most 28 X-data calls (one Latest, three Top, 24 direct); all calls share the adaptive governor, which evenly spaces starts below 10/sec, caps concurrency, prioritizes clicks fairly, reserves known provider quota, and slows further when provider remaining/reset headers require it. The 90k local monthly fallback leaves headroom below the owner's stated 100k plan. Each exact query is cached for 12 minutes, but an older cached metric snapshot cannot claim a live opening.

Each hunt leaves a visible receipt separating live X-data calls from cache hits, accounts selected/returned, unique originals reviewed, live-gate survivors, posts actually content-scored, recommendations, and any budget or partial-provider limit. Every Fresh card recomputes age, public distribution, known reply competition, audience band, and same-author spread when it renders or drafts; an expired result remains available but changes to **cooled** and loses the timing claim. The hero and expanded card name the opportunity class and show observed views, average views/minute since posting, likes/reposts/quotes, replies, and audience multiple when available. Its selection snapshot—including class, public metrics, account source, sourcing priority, observed opportunity, and content fit—is copied into a confirmed `SentRecord`, which makes later outcome analysis possible without pretending the heuristic caused the result.

After content scoring, Fresh Reach computes one comparative opening index: `contentFit × liveObservedOpportunity × boundedMomentum`. `liveObservedOpportunity` includes freshness, thread room, the plateaued reachable-audience factor, author spread, and—only for the breakout/major classes—bounded first-snapshot distribution evidence. A second changed API observation can add at most 12% momentum. Weak content therefore cannot be rescued by a large account or a fast-moving post. On **Best**, the strongest live Fresh candidate is pinned once with a class-specific headline; **Reach** sorts by the same index instead of raw follower count. The UI calls all pace figures observed averages and the index directional, never an impression forecast.

The expanded Replies header exposes **Scan this page** to re-evaluate rendered feed posts without an X-data key. **Search my niche** and **Fresh reach** live under **More tools**, alongside Post ideas, DMs, Growth, and Target accounts. Replies and Comments stay visible as the main views. Today’s goals expand from the pace details, and Fresh Reach diagnostics sit in a collapsed search receipt. Fresh reach is disabled until the user's handle/follower count is known, because “reachable” cannot honestly be inferred without a baseline.

**Measured opportunity momentum.** The selected original posts returned by Find Spots (up to 18) and target-account polling are folded into a compact public-post metric ledger keyed by tweet ID. Two changed observations 5–120 minutes apart produce a short-lived velocity signal from view/like/repost deltas; reply growth is excluded because it is competition. The signal adds at most +12% to **Best-sort order only** and can show `↗ picking up`. It never changes the displayed/recorded fit score, the 0.6 eligibility gate, other sorts, or the learning-loop correlation axis. Identical cached responses do not advance the observation clock; tracks expire after 48 hours and the store caps at 300. Cross-tab updates union by ID and retain the newer observation pair.

**Draft (Claude, Sonnet).** `openDraftFromEl()` / a dock click → `draftFor(req)`. It resolves the promote product, paints the loading panel, and sends `DRAFT_REPLY {author, text, context, angle, style, product, steer, threadLine, opportunityLine}`. `service-worker.ts` reads stored voice + niche and calls `draftReply()` (claude-client.ts) → `claude-sonnet-4-6`, `X_DRAFT_SYSTEM`, `max_tokens 400` normally or 120 for Community Spark, returning plain text (not JSON). The user message carries a **grounding context block** (`draft-context.ts:buildDraftContext` — niche, scorer reason/category, exact anchor/useful move, honest author evidence, relationship context, and observed opportunity context). Fresh-reach grounding tells the drafter to write one self-contained contribution for surrounding readers and never expose the tactic by mentioning account size, Premium, a blue check, reach, or impressions. Target-account drafts now score the post first, create a shared `Opp`, and use this same enriched context/logging path instead of the former bare `{author,text}` call. A monotonically increasing request id prevents a slower old angle/style/steer request from repainting over the newest choice.

**Community Spark style.** `replyStyleControl()` is separate from `angleRow()`: angle chooses what the reply does, while the delivery style changes how the same move lands. It defaults on when `X_COMMUNITY_SPARK_KEY` is absent; the toggle writes an explicit boolean to `chrome.storage.local`, and `storage.onChanged` keeps tabs aligned. New drafts from every reply surface apply the saved choice. Its prompt asks Sonnet to reason internally from the exact anchor and non-obvious implication, then return one 55–170 character reply (at most two short sentences) with compressed insight, precise extension, or natural wit. A question is optional and must invite one answer that improves the thread. Summary, flattery, lecturing, stacked points, forced jokes, manufactured familiarity, engagement bait, and invented experience remain forbidden. If the matching owner has imported an X analytics CSV and a reply-length cohort clears the sample gate, the service worker adds that aggregate range as a correlation-only delivery prior; it explicitly forbids padding or removing the useful point to hit a number. Raw CSV rows are never stored or sent. Confirmed replies store optional `draftStyle` for future outcome analysis without yet changing ranking.

**User-clicked reply action (copy-only).** `runReplyDraftAction(text)` always routes to `handoffReply`. `findPost()` resolves whether the exact article is currently visible. If it is, `startVisiblePostReview()` copies the draft and smoothly centers that matched article with `scrollIntoView`; it does not activate any X control. If it is absent, the source sends `OPEN_REPLY_POST`; the service worker focuses an existing matching status tab or creates the canonical `x.com/<author>/status/<id>` tab. It does not persist the draft, message the destination content script, click Reply, like, or access the composer. The confirmation card still requires explicit **Mark as posted** confirmation.

**State / persistence.** `recordSentReply()` → `logSentReply()` appends a `SentRecord` to `replyLog.sent` (capped `SENT_MAX = 500`), bumps `replyLog.daily`/`total`/`times`, updates the recorded author's last-reply timestamp, adds the post id to `commentedIds` (drives the green `✓ Replied` overlay), and persists the whole `replyLog` to `chrome.storage.local` under `X_REPLY_LOG_KEY`. This happens only after explicit **Mark as posted** confirmation or later API verification; copying/opening does not create a sent record. That timestamp feeds the next pre-draft recommendation. `recordReplyAndNudge()` also updates the normalized-draft dedup list and returns one record-time nudge via `pickReplyNudge`. Pause state persists under `X_PAUSED_KEY`; Community Spark persists under `X_COMMUNITY_SPARK_KEY` and defaults on only when that key is absent. The `seen`/`opps` maps are in-memory per page-session.

## What is honest about it / limits
- **No automated engagement is the keystone.** Every reply flow is copy-only and requires manual X interaction, submission, and confirmation. There is no legacy DOM-insertion toggle.
- **Pace / repetition guards are local and cross-session:** adaptive pressure (`replyPaceStatus` over `replyLog.times` + recommendation lanes), same-author spread (`AUTHOR_REPEAT_TTL` 3 days), and duplicate-reply detection (`isDuplicateReply` over recent normalized drafts). X prohibits bulk/aggressive unsolicited and duplicative or irrelevant replies, but Goobi does not claim to calculate an X reputation score. Follows are paced too (`FOLLOW_MIN_GAP_MS` 20s jittered + `FOLLOW_HOUR_CAP` 15/hr) and `followAuthor` can only follow, never unfollow. Goobi's mood deliberately refuses to celebrate a reply at ease-off.
- **Copy/open handoffs require confirmation.** Copying or opening a post counts nothing until **Mark as posted** or a later API match. The extension does not create a pending record merely because a draft was copied.
- **Score split: model = content/fit only; policy = the user's next move.** Haiku judges the post's text + niche fit and ignores recency/engagement. `recommendReply` then combines observed opportunity and relationship evidence into three inspectable decision scores. Following/follower ratio remains a bounded fallback; exact exchanges and measured engagement-back outrank it. Fixed freshness, competition, and reach curves are product priors, not claims about hidden X weights.
- **Outcome learning IS wired (best-effort).** `runMeasurePass`, called through `maybeRunDailyLearn`, pulls recent replies via `user-replies-v2`, matches each back to a stored `SentRecord` (`matchOutcomes`), and writes real views/likes/replies/reposts into `SentRecord.outcome`; those feed both `aggregateAccounts` and the massive-account keep-list. A manually confirmed reply gets a verification attempt after six minutes; outcome refreshes remain daily, and shortlist admission waits for the two-day settled snapshot. Caveats: this needs a Twttr key. The broader learned reply-queue tilt remains behind its reversible flag and correlation gate; thin or non-predictive data stays neutral.
- **Every opportunity stays manual.** Search-discovered, feed, Target, Comments, and Fresh Reach drafts all use the same copy-only path and require explicit posted confirmation.
- **DOM-scraped, best-effort.** Engagement counts, avatars, verified badges, and display names come from X's DOM (quote-tweet-aware: always the OUTER post). Anything unreadable is simply omitted, never guessed.
- **Per-session cost cap.** `MAX_SCORE_CALLS = 40` bounds Claude spend/ToS exposure per page; hitting it surfaces a one-time toast pointing at ⟳ Rescan (which resets the cap and clears `seen`).

## Key files
- `src/content/x-copilot.ts` — the content-script pipeline: scan/flush, badge/addButton, the dock (`renderList`/`topOpps`/`recommendationFor`), API enrichment, findSpots, drafting, copy/open routing, and reply logging.
- `src/lib/reply-recommendation.ts` — pure three-lane recommendation policy, component evidence, confidence, reasons, and cautions. Covered by counterfactual tests in `scripts/test-reply-recommendation.mjs`.
- `src/lib/fresh-reach.ts` — pure larger-account sourcing plus hard post eligibility and observed-opportunity ordering for just-posted, reachable, uncrowded, account-diverse results. Covered by `scripts/test-fresh-reach.mjs`.
- `src/lib/prompts.ts` — `X_SCORE_SYSTEM`, `X_DRAFT_SYSTEM`, and `REPLY_ANGLES` (the 6 angle directives + labels used for badge tags and the panel chips).
- `src/lib/claude-client.ts` — `scorePosts` (Haiku, JSON), `draftReply` (Sonnet, plain text), `stripDashes` (dash/hyphen safety net), `callDirect`/`rawCall` (BYO-key direct Anthropic fetch).
- `src/background/service-worker.ts` — `SCORE_POSTS` and `DRAFT_REPLY` message handlers (inject stored niche/voice/products), plus `GET_FAVICONS` and the governed `TWTTR_GET`.
- `src/lib/reply-hygiene.ts` — `replyPaceStatus`, `isDuplicateReply`, `normalizeReply`, `pickReplyNudge`, `REPLY_PACE_CAUTION`, and `REPLY_PACE_EASEOFF`. `src/lib/human-pacing.ts` — `humanDelayMs`, `jitterGap`.

## Before you change it
- **Two Claude models, two cost tiers, on purpose:** scoring is Haiku (cheap, batched, JSON, capped at 40 calls/session), drafting is Sonnet (quality, one call per draft, plain text). Don't collapse them or switch the draft to JSON — `draftReply` returns free-form prose precisely because JSON-wrapping reply text is fragile.
- **Eligibility and ordering are different decisions.** `THRESHOLD = 0.6` remains a raw model-fit gate. Eligible posts then run through `recommendReply`; its priority drives Best sort while the lane/reasons drive the row copy. Optional measured per-account learning and momentum are bounded sort-only tilts. Editing the prompt changes relevance eligibility; editing `reply-recommendation.ts` changes where the user's time is spent.
- **The user-click guarantees are load-bearing trust features.** Keep the visible-post path in the current tab, the off-page-only handoff, Fresh reach's manual-only boundary, explicit confirmation, and hourly/duplicate/spread nudges. Never auto-submit or add word-by-word synthetic typing.
- **X's virtualized timeline + closed shadow roots:** dedup is by status id, overlays are idempotent and re-applied from the `seen` cache on remount, and an open overlay reads its latest WeakMap model instead of retaining stale recommendation data. DOM writes are conditional because the body observer sees article descendants. `findPost` is strict id-only (never a same-author lookalike), and panel inputs live in closed shadow roots with `trapKeys` so X's single-key shortcuts don't hijack typing. Respect these when adding DOM logic.
- **Tests:** `scripts/test-reply-recommendation.mjs` asserts reachable-vs-mega, known-vs-missing competition, crowding, exact connections, poor-fit protection, peers, thin reply-back evidence, freshness, and author diversity. The clipboard/new-tab/confirmation wiring still relies on `tsc --noEmit` + build + manual on-page testing.
- **`SentRecord.outcome` IS written and read** — `runMeasurePass` writes it (`x-copilot.ts:2198`) and `aggregateAccounts` reads it (`learn-stats.ts:108,121`). It's sparse (needs a Twttr key + ≥4 settled outcomes per account before a measured score shows), but it is NOT dead weight — don't re-stub it. This is the data that makes the audit's "measure-first" tests runnable on data already in hand.
